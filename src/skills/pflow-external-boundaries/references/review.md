# Boundary review

Review each claim against the reachable invocation and selected binding/configuration, not just its source range or expected keyword. Check internal versus non-owned classification, active versus unused configuration, composed versus unresolved values, technology/product distinctions, secret exclusion, waiting semantics and source roles.

Reject invented complete URLs, tables inferred from class names, guessed broker/cloud products, unbound imports promoted to calls, and duplicated library/system nodes for one operation. Check that significant library details are concise and no non-owned body is expanded. Unknown callable targets must not acquire external metadata merely because a plugin is involved.

Compare IDs/edges and ownership with M5-02. Preserve confirmed relationships; do not add producer/consumer execution, expansion or speculative links. Validate schema/semantics/Markdown, then perform source-meaning review separately.

Use small raw repositories plus confirmed handoffs for blind evaluation without exposing intended metadata. Where authored controlled fixtures exist, inspect actual invocation arguments and change active config/bindings to test sensitivity. Separate those deterministic fixture/oracle checks from AI forward-run results. No live services, target bootstrap or third-party execution is needed. Limited samples do not establish general boundary-analysis recall.
